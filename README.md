# 🏨 HotelHup

### Hotel Property Management System

HotelHup is a web-based hotel management system designed to help hotel teams manage their daily operations in one place. It brings together property, room, guest, reservation, folio, and payment management, reducing the need to rely on disconnected tools and manual workflows.
## ✨ Key Features

- 🏨 **Property & Room Management** — Manage hotel properties, settings, room types, room availability, and room status.
- 📅 **Reservation Lifecycle** — Create, update, confirm, and cancel reservations; manage no-shows and reservation history.
- 🛎️ **Front Desk Operations** — Assign and release rooms, check guests in and out, extend stays, and process early checkouts.
- 👥 **Guest Management** — Maintain guest records and view their reservation history.
- 💳 **Folio & Payment Management** — Track folio charges, record payments, process refunds, and close or reopen folios.
- 🧹 **Hotel Operations** — Assign and track housekeeping tasks, manage hotel services, and record expenses.
- 💰 **Pricing, Policies & Reporting** — Manage rate plans, taxes, cancellation and deposit policies, and reports for occupancy, revenue, arrivals, departures, and more.
- 🔐 **User Access Management** — Manage user accounts, roles, authentication, and permission-related access.
